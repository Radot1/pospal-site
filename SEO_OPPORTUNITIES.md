## Content Quality Rules
To avoid AI slop, each SEO page should include specific operational detail.

Required page pattern:
1. Who this is for.
2. Who this is not for.
3. What you need before downloading.
4. What happens in the first 30 minutes.
5. What POSPal includes.
6. What POSPal does not do.
7. Download for Windows.
8. Then follow the relevant guide.

Do not write generic sections like:
- "Benefits of digital transformation."
- "Why restaurants need modern technology."
- "Improve efficiency and customer satisfaction."

Prefer concrete Greek hospitality details:
- Windows PC.
- USB thermal printer.
- Phone/tablet used by staff.
- Tables, takeaway, simple mode, QR menu.
- 30-day trial with no card and no personal information.
- POSPal is not a fiscal POS and not a cash register.

## Measurement Plan

- Track GA4 events:

Suggested review window:
- Wait at least 28 days after deployment before judging SEO impact.
- Do not rewrite the same page repeatedly inside the same measurement window unless there is a clear technical breakage.
