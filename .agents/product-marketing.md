# Product Marketing Context

*Last updated: 2026-08-02*

## Product Overview

**One-liner:** POSPal is a locally installed ordering and kitchen-workflow tool for Greek hospitality businesses that want direct, transparent access without a distributor.

**Canonical public descriptor:** `POSPal.gr — σύστημα παραγγελιοληψίας και ροής κουζίνας για καφέ και εστιατόρια στην Ελλάδα.`

**What it does:** POSPal combines table service, a simple mode without tables, kitchen order flow, and a QR menu. It installs on a Windows computer and works alongside the business's existing fiscal system.

**Product category:** Restaurant and hospitality order-taking software; PDA ordering; kitchen workflow; QR menu.

**Product type:** Locally installed Windows software with a direct monthly subscription and hosted supporting services. Do not describe it as fully self-hosted until the complete architecture supports that claim.

**Business model:** EUR 23.90 per month including VAT after a 30-day free trial. No card or personal information is required to begin the trial. Cancel anytime.

## Target Audience

**Target companies:** Greek cafes, restaurants, beach bars, food trucks, and seasonal hospitality businesses. The strongest initial fit is an independently operated venue that wants to manage its own setup.

**Decision-makers:** Owners, managers, and operators who control tooling and budgets.

**Primary use case:** Replace or avoid distributor-led ordering software with a direct, self-managed ordering workflow that is inexpensive and clearly documented.

**Jobs to be done:**

- Take orders from a phone or tablet and move them into a clear kitchen workflow.
- Run tables, counter/takeaway orders, and a QR menu from one product catalogue.
- Install and operate the system without a sales call, middleman, or opaque maintenance plan.

**Use cases:**

- An existing PDA/order-taking user looking for a simpler and more transparent alternative.
- A new or seasonal venue that needs a low-risk setup before opening or peak season.
- An owner who wants software on their own Windows computer and is prepared to follow step-by-step guides.

## Problems & Pain Points

**Core problem:** Hospitality operators need practical order-taking software but often encounter unclear pricing, distributor dependency, fragmented tools, and weak self-service setup material.

**Why alternatives fall short:**

- Pricing and maintenance terms may be unclear.
- A distributor or sales conversation may be required before starting.
- Table service, simple ordering, and QR menu may be split across products or packages.
- Setup guidance may not give the owner enough confidence to install independently.

**What it costs them:** More setup time, avoidable recurring cost, dependency on third parties, and operational uncertainty before a live shift.

**Emotional tension:** The owner wants control and a lower-cost route, but worries that self-installation will fail when the venue is busy.

## Competitive Landscape

Public marketing copy must not name competitors.

**Direct:** Distributor-sold PDA and hospitality ordering systems with maintenance plans or opaque pricing.

**Secondary:** QR-only menu tools, generic ordering software, and broader fiscal POS suites.

**Indirect:** Paper tickets, verbal ordering, messaging apps, spreadsheets, or continuing with a system that barely works.

## Differentiation

**Key differentiators:**

- Transparent EUR 23.90 monthly price including VAT.
- Thirty-day trial without a card or personal information.
- Direct subscription without a distributor or maintenance plan.
- Table mode, simple mode, kitchen flow, and QR menu in one package.
- Locally installed Windows application with a self-managed setup path.
- Explicitly works alongside the existing fiscal POS rather than claiming to replace it.

**How we do it differently:** POSPal lets an operator download first, install with strong guidance, evaluate the complete workflow, and subscribe directly.

**Why that is better:** The buyer can understand the cost, requirements, setup process, and product boundary before making a commitment.

**Why customers choose us:** Working hypothesis: control, transparent cost, modern workflow, and the ability to start without a salesperson. This needs validation through real customer interviews or support conversations.

## Objections

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| Will this replace my fiscal POS or cash register? | No. POSPal covers ordering and kitchen workflow and runs alongside the existing fiscal system. |
| Will it work with my equipment? | The site must state the Windows, printer, device, and network requirements before download. Exact compatibility claims must be verified. |
| Can I install it without a technician? | The product is intended to support self-installation through Guides 0-3, troubleshooting, and support escalation. |
| What happens if setup fails? | Provide clear troubleshooting and a visible support path; do not promise response times or channels that cannot be delivered. |

**Anti-persona:** A buyer seeking a fiscal POS, cash register, myDATA solution, managed hardware bundle, guaranteed onsite installation, or an entirely mobile/iOS host setup.

## Switching Dynamics

**Push:** High or unclear costs, distributor dependency, maintenance plans, fragmented tools, and an outdated workflow.

**Pull:** Transparent pricing, direct download, no-card trial, all-in-one ordering workflow, and clear onboarding.

**Habit:** The existing system is already installed, staff know it, and changing an operational tool feels risky.

**Anxiety:** Hardware compatibility, printer setup, data/menu setup, support availability, and whether the first live shift will work.
