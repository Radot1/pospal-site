# Content Decisions (POSPal Landing)

This file captures copy decisions for the landing page. Internal notes are in English; approved public copy remains Greek.

## Positioning
- Product is **not** a fiscal POS.
- Product is a **system for order-taking/workflow** ("σύστημα παραγγελιοληψίας").
- Use "σύστημα παραγγελιοληψίας" wording in hero instead of a direct "not fiscal POS" statement.
- Explicit "not fiscal POS" note will live outside the hero (FAQ + pricing footnote + footer).
- Lead with modern UI + staff-first experience.
- Emphasize live QR menu, mobile-optimized (no PDF/zoom).
- Emphasize order capture when the device goes out of Wi-Fi range (store + send on reconnect).
- Emphasize subscription flexibility (cancel anytime).
- Emphasize: 30-day free trial, no card, no account.
