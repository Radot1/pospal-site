# POSPal Website Redesign Brief

## Goal

Build a clear, download-first Greek website that makes POSPal easy to understand, install, and start using without a distributor or sales call.

## Audience

Primary audience: Greek hospitality owners and managers who already use, or are actively looking for, order-taking software and want:

- transparent, low monthly pricing;
- direct access without a middleman;
- software installed on their own Windows computer;
- tables, simple ordering, kitchen flow, and QR menu in one product;
- strong instructions for setting everything up themselves.

Secondary audience: new and seasonal cafes, restaurants, beach bars, and food trucks that need a fast, low-risk setup.

POSPal is not for visitors seeking a fiscal POS, cash register, myDATA solution, managed hardware package, or fully serviced onsite installation.

## Positioning

POSPal is a locally installed, self-managed ordering and kitchen-workflow tool that runs alongside the business's existing fiscal system.

Do not describe POSPal as fully self-hosted until the complete product architecture supports that claim. Lead with transparent pricing, ownership of the setup, and no middleman rather than calling it "cheap."

## Conversion Path

1. Understand what POSPal is within five seconds.
2. Confirm that the existing equipment is suitable.
3. See real product screens and a concrete shift workflow.
4. Download `POSPal-win-Setup.exe`.
5. Start the correct installation guide immediately.
6. Complete the first menu and first working shift.
7. Subscribe after the 30-day trial.

Primary CTA: download for Windows. Secondary CTA: installation and setup guides. Demo content is supporting proof only.

## Lean Website Structure

- Home
- Download
- One canonical pricing page
- Guides
- Support
- Consolidated SEO landing pages outside the primary navigation

## Onboarding Requirement

The redesign should not launch until these guides are complete:

- Guide 0: printer and equipment preparation
- Guide 1: Windows installation
- Guide 2: first menu and QR setup
- Guide 3: first real shift

Every guide needs prerequisites, estimated time, numbered steps, real screenshots, expected results, troubleshooting, support escalation, and one clear next step. Do not advertise unfinished guides.

## Design Principles

- Use real POSPal screens and Greek hospitality workflows.
- Show the path from staff device to order flow and kitchen printer.
- Prefer specific operational details over generic benefit claims.
- Avoid stock photography, fake dashboards, excessive cards, generic gradients, and fabricated proof.
- Keep public copy Greek-only, concise, factual, and monotonic.
- Preserve existing search equity through deliberate URL consolidation and redirects.

## Work Order

1. Finalize product-marketing context and terminology.
2. Capture the current SEO, URL, and analytics baseline.
3. Define the sitemap and onboarding curriculum.
4. Produce two or three distinct homepage concepts.
5. Review concepts for conversion and accessibility.
6. Draft Greek copy and obtain approval.
7. Implement on `redesign/new-website`.
8. Run browser, accessibility, link, conversion, and SEO migration QA.
9. Deploy and measure for at least 28 days.
