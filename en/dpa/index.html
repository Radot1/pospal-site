This DPA forms part of the B2B contract only when POSPal processes personal data on the merchant's behalf and it has been incorporated into a binding order/contract or accepted through a recorded written or electronic action. Continued use alone is not presented as standalone proof of acceptance.

1. Roles and scope

The merchant is controller and POSPal is processor for personal data the merchant chooses to send to a public Customer Menu, support, or another cloud feature on its behalf. POSPal is an independent controller for licensing, security, billing, and its legal duties as described in the Privacy Notice.

3. Instructions/confidentiality

POSPal processes only on documented instructions expressed through feature use and contract, unless law requires otherwise. If an instruction infringes the GDPR or other applicable data-protection law, POSPal informs merchant before acting unless law prohibits notice. Authorised persons owe confidentiality. Merchant gives no unlawful instructions and avoids special-category/payment data.

4. Security

Verifiable measures for this release include HTTPS in transit to cloud APIs, hashing of selected identifiers, minimisation/redaction of selected diagnostics, account/repository access controls, and logging of selected important events. The incident procedure and current internal technical and organisational measures schedule are documented separately and reviewed. No certification, complete environment separation, or absolute security is claimed. Merchant secures devices, LAN, accounts, PINs, and backups.

5. Subprocessors/transfers

Merchant gives general written authorisation for the Subprocessor List only when this DPA has been bindingly incorporated into the contract. For a material new processor, the list is updated and notice is sent to the available billing/email address normally 30 days beforehand where practicable, allowing a substantiated objection. If no reasonable solution exists, either party may discontinue the affected feature under the contract. Appropriate contractual duties and verified lawful transfer mechanisms must be in place before the relevant processing.

6. Compliance assistance

Taking account of processing, POSPal reasonably assists with data-subject requests, security, breach notification, DPIAs, and consultation. Merchant remains responsible for decisions/communications. POSPal will notify merchant without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal-data breach involving its data; initial notice may be supplemented as facts are confirmed.

7. Return/deletion

On termination, merchant exports local data using available means. At merchant's choice through support, cloud data is returned where an export format is available and then deleted, or deleted without return. Deletion covers copies controlled by POSPal and backups as their cycles expire, unless law requires retention. If no choice is given, the retention schedule applies. Enforcement is currently manual quarterly review, not automated.

8. Audits

Available compliance information is supplied on reasonable request. One reasonable annual audit may be agreed under confidentiality, without access to other customers, secrets, or vulnerabilities and without undue disruption. Additional audit may follow a material incident or authority demand.
