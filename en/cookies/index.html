This policy covers pospal.gr. App data is separately described in the Privacy Notice.

1. Choices and withdrawal

Necessary storage remembers your choice. Analytics, marketing, and external media controlled by POSPal activate only after the matching positive choice. Accept all and reject all are presented with equal prominence. Select “Cookie settings” in the footer or floating button to change or withdraw. You can also clear cookies/local storage in your browser.

3. Technical enforcement

Consent is managed by a locally served POSPal script. Google Analytics, Google Ads, Clarity, and YouTube remain disconnected until the relevant positive choice. Withdrawal updates Google Consent Mode and the Clarity consent API, disables the corresponding signals, and disconnects embedded videos.

4. Updates

Third-party names and durations may change by provider, geography, device, and configuration. The inventory is checked for each legal release and through a browser test of the build intended for publication. Questions: support@pospal.gr.
