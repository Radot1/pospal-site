# Freeze Launch Inventory And Gate SEO Expansion

The redesign launch candidate freezes the public inventory around the locked conversion core (`/`, `/download/`, `/guides/`) and the selected SEO acquisition layer already justified by Search Console data. SEO growth will continue after launch, but only through the SEO expansion gate: a new page needs a real intent cluster, a distinct funnel job, a quality implementation, and a small tested batch. This prevents legacy URL restoration and keyword fragments from scaling the site into thin content while preserving the current search-equity work.

`/guides/` remains the only public onboarding and support destination for launch; support pages, individual guide URLs, QR/menu routes, demo pages, privacy pages, and additional legacy URLs remain redirected or deferred unless post-launch evidence justifies reopening them.

Locked pages may receive only narrowly scoped first-party launch blocker fixes after lock. This exception covers accessibility, tap-target, overflow, broken-route, and critical crawlability repairs. It does not allow new content, SEO rewrites, visual redesign, or route expansion. Third-party accessibility findings from the embedded YouTube guide player are documented as exceptions while the video remains necessary for launch onboarding.
