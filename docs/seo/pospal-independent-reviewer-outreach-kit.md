## Purpose

This kit helps POSPal invite one genuine customer, reviewer, publication, video creator, or relevant partner to inspect the product independently. It does not request a positive review, prescribe a conclusion, or turn supplied facts into independent proof.

The recipient chooses what to test, controls the environment and evidence, and decides whether and how to publish. POSPal may correct factual errors but must accept negative findings, limitations, declined publication, and no response.

## Reviewer handoff checklist

Before sending material, agree on the following:

- The reviewer chooses the fact IDs and test scope.
- The reviewer controls the device, network, test data, captures, notes, conclusion, and publication decision.
- Supplied access, assistance, discounts, or other benefits are disclosed where relevant.
- Observed results are separated from POSPal-supplied facts and personal interpretation.
- Failed steps, limitations, and untested areas are retained.
- No real guest data, private venue data, customer contact details, or unrelated correspondence is collected for the review.
- POSPal does not review wording beyond correcting demonstrable factual errors.
- A private test, draft, or conversation is not labelled independent proof.
- `FAC-IND-001` advances only after a qualifying source publishes or retains its own attributable conclusion under its own control.

## Send gate

Outreach may begin only when:

- one genuine recipient has been selected;
- the recipient is relevant to the chosen narrow scope;
- the public-safe fact pack has been rechecked;
- any supplied access or assistance can be disclosed honestly;
- the private contact record is stored outside Git;
- no positive conclusion, publication, link, or endorsement has been promised.
