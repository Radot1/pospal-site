Status: Empty template
Purpose: capture real discovery language without contaminating the frozen benchmark

## Privacy and evidence rules

- Ask permission before saving a shared conversation or screenshot.
- Redact names, email addresses, phone numbers, account identifiers, and unrelated conversation history.
- Do not commit screenshots or browser captures to this repository.
- Record customer wording exactly; do not rewrite it as marketing copy.
- Label every report `customer_reported` until independently verified.
- Do not add a new prompt to benchmark version `1.0`. Candidate prompts require a later version and remain a separate time series.
