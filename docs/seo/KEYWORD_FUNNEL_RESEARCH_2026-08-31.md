# POSPal Single-Funnel Keyword Research

Date: 2026-08-31
Status: Measured research record; no website or Google Ads changes made

## Purpose

Select exactly one educational topic and one commercial-intent topic for the acquisition path into the existing POSPal money page:

```text
Educational
→ Commercial
→ /systima-paraggeliolipsias.html
→ /download/
→ /guides/
```

This is deliberately a single funnel. It does not propose additional branches, pages, or content clusters.

## Decision

Use the existing pages in this order:

```text
/pda-ti-einai.html
→ /times.html
→ /systima-paraggeliolipsias.html
→ /download/
→ /guides/
```

### Selected educational topic

**`PDA τι είναι στην εστίαση`**

Destination: `/pda-ti-einai.html`

Why it won:

- Google Keyword Planner reports **500 average monthly searches** for the normalized close variant `pda τι ειναι`.
- The cluster is stable: **0% three-month change** and **0% year-over-year change**.
- Google Ads competition is **Low**, indexed value **1**.
- Search Console independently confirms the demand: the exact query `pda τι είναι` generated **1,326 impressions** in the recorded three-month period.
- The definition page generated **5,143 impressions and 74 clicks** in that same period.
- The alternative educational topics were materially smaller or unreportable.

### Selected commercial-intent topic

**`Σύστημα παραγγελιοληψίας: τιμές, κόστος και τι περιλαμβάνεται`**

Destination: `/times.html`

Why it won:

- Google Keyword Planner reports **50 average monthly searches** for the normalized close variant `συστημα παραγγελιοληψιασ τιμεσ`.
- The cluster is stable: **0% three-month change** and **0% year-over-year change**.
- Google Ads competition is **High**, indexed value **67**.
- The top-of-page bid range is **€1.19–€5.91**, showing commercial advertiser interest.
- `Πρόγραμμα παραγγελιοληψίας εστίασης` also has a 50-search representative variant, but it overlaps directly with the category intent assigned to `/systima-paraggeliolipsias.html`.
- The pricing topic is therefore the strongest distinct commercial bridge into the money page without creating intent cannibalization.
- `Κόστος`, `πώς να επιλέξω`, and `καλύτερο` did not produce comparable measurable demand as standalone exact clusters.

### Reporting limitation

The account has no running campaign. Google therefore showed ranges such as `0–10`, `10–100`, and `100–1k` in the interface and displayed the notice that more detailed statistics require running a campaign. No campaign was run.

The downloaded Keyword Planner CSV normalized some reportable close variants to point estimates such as 50 or 500. Individual monthly counts were not populated. This record uses:

- the CSV point estimate where Google supplied one;
- the interface range where the CSV left the field empty;
- three-month change for short-term direction;
- year-over-year change as the available 12-month trend signal;
- `Not reported` rather than inventing a value where Google withheld the metric.

Google handled accents and final-sigma spellings inconsistently. For example:

- the literal accented seed `PDA τι είναι` appeared as `0–10`;
- the normalized close variant `pda τι ειναι` appeared as 500;
- the normalized form `συστημα παραγγελιοληψιασ τιμεσ` appeared as 50 while some correctly spelled seed forms remained unreported.

For this reason, decisions are based on intent clusters and normalized close variants, not on a literal comparison of accented and unaccented spellings.

## Funnel responsibilities

### 1. `/pda-ti-einai.html`

- Answer the PDA definition immediately.
- Frame the term specifically in the context of Greek hospitality.
- Use the related variants naturally in the same page.
- Primary next step for this funnel: `/times.html`.

### 2. `/times.html`

- Answer the market question: what does a system like this cost and what affects the total cost?
- Explain the POSPal price, trial, inclusions, cancellation, and absence of a per-PDA fee.
- Distinguish software cost from equipment decisions without making unqualified compatibility claims.
- Primary next step: `/systima-paraggeliolipsias.html`.

### 3. `/systima-paraggeliolipsias.html`

- Own the generic system/program category decision.
- Explain product fit, workflow, requirements, boundaries, and the exact POSPal offer.
- Primary next step: `/download/`.

### 4. `/download/`

- Hand off to `POSPal-win-Setup.exe`.
- Preserve the 30-day free-trial promise with no card or personal information required.

### 5. `/guides/`

- Continue from download toward installation, setup, first use, and troubleshooting.
- Act as post-interest and post-download evidence that the product can be implemented and supported.

## What this research does not justify

- No new educational URL.
- No new “how to choose” page.
- No new “best system” page.
- No equipment article as the first funnel entry.
- No separate page targeting the generic `πρόγραμμα παραγγελιοληψίας εστίασης` query; that intent belongs to the money page.
- No additional funnel branches until this single sequence is implemented and measured.
