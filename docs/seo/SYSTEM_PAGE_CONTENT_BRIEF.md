# POSPal Commercial System Page Brief

Status: Draft for review — no live-page changes approved or made  
Target page: `/systima-paraggeliolipsias.html`  
Last updated: 2026-08-29

## Decision

This page should become POSPal's main commercial decision page for an independent hospitality owner who is willing to install and test the software personally.

It should still explain the product accurately, but its first job is no longer to document every part of the system. Its first job is to make the right owner think:

> Αυτό είναι για το δικό μου κατάστημα. Ξέρω τι κάνει, τι χρειάζομαι, πόσο κοστίζει και μπορώ να το δοκιμάσω τώρα.

The page must serve two compatible traffic sources:

- owners searching Google for a restaurant ordering system or program;
- owners arriving from the new video ad and expecting the same direct, self-service offer.

Primary conversion: open `/download/` and download `POSPal-win-Setup.exe`.  
Secondary conversion: open the installation/setup guides when more confidence is needed.

## Evidence and assumptions

### Measured

- Search Console review for the recent comparison period showed this page with 968 impressions and 1 click.
- Its observed click-through rate was therefore approximately 0.10%.
- The current HTML contains 1,054 main-content words, one H1, ten H2s, nine contextual internal links, two real product images, a canonical URL, and FAQ structured data.
- The current title is 56 characters and the meta description is 153 characters.

### User-provided business fact

- POSPal is not receiving meaningful new software trials as it used to.
- The intended customer is the younger-in-mind, DIY owner/operator who is prepared to download, install, and evaluate the software without a middleman.

### Estimated strategic inference

- The page has enough search visibility to justify improving its search-result message and commercial argument now.
- The low click-through rate does not prove a single cause. Likely contributors are ranking position, snippet appeal, and a weak match between the searcher's buying problem and the promise visible in the result.
- The best primary intent is choosing a self-managed ordering system for a Greek hospitality venue, not learning what a PDA is.

Average page position, query-level conversion rate, installer completion, and page-to-trial conversion are currently N/A for this brief.

## Audience

### Primary reader

An owner, manager, or operator of a Greek café, restaurant, beach bar, food truck, takeaway, or seasonal venue who:

- controls the software and equipment decision;
- wants to avoid a distributor or opaque maintenance agreement;
- prefers a clear monthly price;
- is willing to follow instructions and set up the software;
- wants staff to take orders from ordinary phones or tablets;
- wants to reuse compatible Windows thermal printers where possible;
- wants ordering, kitchen workflow, table/simple modes, and a QR menu together;
- wants to test the real workflow before paying.

### Likely buying triggers

- opening or preparing a venue for the season;
- replacing an expensive, outdated, or distributor-dependent system;
- adding wireless ordering without buying dedicated PDA hardware;
- adding a QR menu without another separate subscription;
- trying to reduce unnecessary equipment and recurring costs.

### Anti-persona

This page is not for someone seeking a cash register, fiscal POS, myDATA solution, guaranteed onsite installation, managed hardware bundle, or a fully mobile/iOS host system.

## Priority fixes

### P0 — required before implementation

1. Put the owner offer above the technical explanation: self-installation, no middleman, 30-day trial, exact monthly price, and download.
2. Make the hero repeat the video ad's promise so paid visitors do not experience a message break.
3. Add a dedicated price-and-risk section instead of leaving the offer in a single compact line.
4. Reorder the page around the buyer's questions: Is it for me? What does it do? What do I need? What does it cost? What could stop it working? How do I try it?
5. Keep one dominant action throughout: `Κατέβασε για Windows`.

### P1 — high value

1. Compress the separate reconnect/offline section into an honest FAQ answer and link to the detailed wireless-ordering page.
2. Bring ordinary phones/tablets, compatible existing printers, and included QR menu into the first product-proof section.
3. Add a clear self-fit and anti-fit section to prevent fiscal-POS and managed-installation leads from misunderstanding the product.
4. Use stronger contextual links to price, setup requirements, and the installer handoff.
5. Add trial, printer compatibility, setup, and cancellation questions to the visible FAQ and keep its schema synchronized.

### P2 — later supporting work

1. Add valid SoftwareApplication/Product and Organization data through the dedicated schema phase.
2. Add authentic external proof only when it exists: real reviews, directory profiles, trade mentions, or customer evidence.
3. Consider a founder proof element after the founder identity and presentation are approved for the site.

## Recommended page structure and draft copy direction

This is a content brief, not the final approved copy. It deliberately defines the argument before polishing every sentence.

### 1. Hero — identify the product and remove purchase risk

**Signal**

> Για καφέ, εστιατόρια, beach bar και food truck

**H1**

> Σύστημα παραγγελιοληψίας χωρίς όλη την ιστορία.

**Lead**

> Εγκαθιστάς το POSPal σε υπολογιστή Windows και η ομάδα σου περνά παραγγελίες από κινητά ή tablet στο τοπικό δίκτυο. Οι παραγγελίες φτάνουν στη ροή κουζίνας, ενώ το QR μενού περιλαμβάνεται στην ίδια συνδρομή.

**Primary CTA**

> Κατέβασε για Windows

**CTA microcopy**

> 30 ημέρες δωρεάν · Χωρίς κάρτα ή προσωπικά στοιχεία

**Price line**

> Μετά τη δοκιμή: 23,90 € τον μήνα με ΦΠΑ. Ακύρωση όποτε θέλεις.

**Secondary text link**

> Δες τι χρειάζεσαι για το στήσιμο

This link should jump to the requirements section on the same page. The guide link can follow after the requirements instead of competing with the first download CTA.

### 2. Self-fit — qualify the DIY owner

**H2**

> Θέλεις να το στήσεις μόνος σου; Εδώ ξεκινάς.

**Purpose**

Make the self-service position explicit and turn “no middleman” into control rather than just a price complaint.

**Points to cover**

- Download first; no sales call before the trial.
- Use a Windows computer you control.
- Connect ordinary phones or tablets through the venue's accessible local network.
- Test the real menu, devices, and printing flow before subscribing.
- Follow the setup guides when needed.

**Boundary note**

> Αν ψάχνεις ταμειακή ή φορολογικό POS, το POSPal δεν είναι αυτό. Καλύπτει την παραγγελιοληψία και τη ροή κουζίνας δίπλα στο σύστημα που ήδη χρησιμοποιείς.

### 3. Complete workflow — show what the owner gets

**H2**

> Από το κινητό στην κουζίνα, χωρίς ξεχωριστή εφαρμογή στο κινητό.

Use four or five compact proof blocks:

1. **Κινητά και tablet** — the staff opens the local link or connection QR; devices must communicate with the Windows computer on the local network.
2. **Απλή λειτουργία ή τραπέζια** — support counter/takeaway workflows and table service from the same product catalogue.
3. **Ροή κουζίνας** — orders reach the central Windows installation and can be routed by category.
4. **Συμβατοί θερμικοί εκτυπωτές** — reuse printers that are installed and work correctly in Windows; verify with a real test print.
5. **QR μενού** — publish a customer menu using the same catalogue; internet is required for publication.

Keep both real screenshots here. Use them as proof, not decoration.

### 4. Setup path — make self-installation feel concrete

**H2**

> Το κατεβάζεις, το στήνεις και δοκιμάζεις μια πραγματική παραγγελία.

**Three steps**

1. **Κατέβασε το POSPal** — install the Windows x64 package on the venue's computer.
2. **Σύνδεσε τη ροή σου** — prepare the catalogue, choose simple/table mode, connect staff devices, and configure required printers.
3. **Κάνε δοκιμή στο κατάστημα** — send an actual test order and check the real printed result before a live shift.

Place the guide link here:

> Άνοιξε τον οδηγό εγκατάστασης

The guide remains secondary and confidence-building. It does not replace the download action.

### 5. Requirements — answer compatibility before it becomes fear

**H2**

> Τι χρειάζεσαι πριν ξεκινήσεις.

Keep the current requirements ledger, but shorten its language:

- Windows x64 computer;
- accessible local network for staff devices;
- phone or tablet with a browser;
- compatible thermal printer already installed in Windows, if printing is required;
- internet for QR publication, licence checks, and application updates;
- the venue's separate fiscal system.

Add a contextual link to the detailed wireless-ordering page after the local-network item.

### 6. Price and trial — give the commercial decision its own section

**H2**

> Το δοκιμάζεις πρώτα. Πληρώνεις μόνο αν σου κάνει.

**Offer block**

> 30 ημέρες δωρεάν  
> Χωρίς κάρτα ή προσωπικά στοιχεία  
> 23,90 € τον μήνα με ΦΠΑ μετά τη δοκιμή  
> Ακύρωση όποτε θέλεις

**Primary CTA**

> Κατέβασε για Windows

**Supporting link**

> Δες αναλυτικά την τιμή και τη συνδρομή

The price page should own detailed billing questions. This section should still contain enough information to let an owner continue directly to download.

### 7. Honest limitations — keep trust without leading with edge cases

Do not hide the current technical truth, but move it later in the decision sequence.

Cover these points briefly:

- Staff devices need access to the venue's local network.
- Temporary same-device queued actions are not a shared cloud/offline queue.
- Printer compatibility must be checked with the real Windows installation and a test print.
- POSPal does not replace the fiscal system.

The detailed reconnect explanation should live in the FAQ and the wireless-ordering page rather than occupying a full early section.

### 8. FAQ — answer purchase objections

Recommended questions:

1. `Μπορώ να το εγκαταστήσω χωρίς τεχνικό;`
2. `Χρειάζεται εφαρμογή στα κινητά ή τα tablet;`
3. `Μπορώ να χρησιμοποιήσω τους εκτυπωτές που έχω;`
4. `Τι χρειάζεται να λειτουργεί στο τοπικό δίκτυο;`
5. `Τι γίνεται αν χαθεί προσωρινά η σύνδεση;`
6. `Το QR μενού περιλαμβάνεται στην τιμή;`
7. `Τι γίνεται μετά τις 30 δωρεάν ημέρες;`
8. `Το POSPal αντικαθιστά την ταμειακή ή το φορολογικό POS;`

Any FAQ schema must match the visible answers exactly.

### 9. Final CTA — repeat the low-risk decision

**H2**

> Δοκίμασέ το στο δικό σου κατάστημα για 30 ημέρες.

**Supporting line**

> Κατέβασε το POSPal για Windows, στήσε την πραγματική σου ροή και αποφάσισε αφού το χρησιμοποιήσεις.

**Primary CTA**

> Κατέβασε για Windows

**Microcopy**

> Χωρίς κάρτα · Χωρίς προσωπικά στοιχεία · Μετά 23,90 €/μήνα με ΦΠΑ

## What to keep, move, and reduce

### Keep

- the current URL and canonical;
- the real desktop-order and QR-menu screenshots;
- the two operation modes;
- the local-network explanation;
- honest printer requirements;
- the product's fiscal boundary;
- FAQ schema, synchronized with visible answers;
- links to pricing, guides, wireless ordering, and venue-specific pages.

### Move later

- the detailed temporary-connection behaviour;
- the long product-boundary block;
- links to every supporting SEO path.

### Reduce or merge

- merge the modes, device route, printing, and QR sections into a more compact complete-workflow argument;
- replace the generic “go to the answer” directory with contextual links placed beside the relevant objection;
- reduce repeated mentions of `Windows` and `φορολογικό POS` once each requirement and boundary is already clear.

## Conversion risks to avoid

- Do not say every existing printer will work.
- Do not imply staff devices work without an accessible local network.
- Do not present temporary same-device queueing as full offline operation.
- Do not imply POSPal replaces the fiscal POS or cash register.
- Do not invent testimonials, savings figures, integrations, or setup guarantees.
- Do not send the first-click visitor into guides before explaining why the product is worth installing.
- Do not add more competing CTAs.
