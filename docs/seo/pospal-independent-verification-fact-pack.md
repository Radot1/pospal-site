## Use rule

This pack separates supplied product facts from observations an independent source must make for itself. Sending it to someone does not create independent evidence. `FAC-IND-001` advances only when an external source publishes or retains its own attributable conclusion after its own review.

The recipient must be free to disagree, report limitations, decline publication, and choose its own wording. POSPal may correct factual errors but must not trade payment, access, discounts, or other benefits for a positive conclusion without conspicuous disclosure.

## What the current evidence does not prove

Do not infer any of the following from this pack:

- support for every Windows version or processor architecture;
- support for a named phone, tablet, browser, router, network layout, printer, driver, or interface;
- specific port, firewall, IP-address, discovery, offline, retry, recovery, or sync behavior;
- installation time, response time, uptime, order volume, error reduction, revenue, cost saving, or service-speed outcome;
- a customer count, market share, rating, award, certification, compliance status, or recommendation;
- a fiscal integration or replacement for a cash register;
- onsite setup, done-for-you configuration, or a support-response SLA;
- an independent endorsement merely because the reviewer received software, information, or assistance.

## Independent reproduction path

A reviewer who wants to establish independent operational evidence should control the device, environment, test steps, raw captures, and published conclusion.

1. Record the date, reviewer/publisher, disclosure terms, and whether access or any benefit was supplied.
2. Download the installer only from the official latest-release link.
3. Record the release tag, asset name, byte size, SHA-256, signature result, and download timestamp.
4. Record the exact Windows edition, version, build, architecture, installation state, and permissions.
5. Record install prompts, warnings, firewall changes, restart behavior, first launch, and update behavior.
6. If testing staff-device flow, record the exact device, OS, client/browser, router/network conditions, and a traceable test order.
7. If testing disconnection or internet outage, define the exact boundary and check loss, duplication, recovery, and manual steps.
8. If testing printing, identify make, model, firmware, driver, interface, paper settings, Greek-character output, and retry behavior; retain authentic output.
9. Separate observed results from POSPal-supplied facts and from the reviewer's interpretation.
10. State limitations, failed steps, and untested areas as prominently as successful steps.

The internal [`PRF-2026-001`](proof-records/PRF-2026-001.md) offers a more detailed checkpoint list. Share only a public-safe copy; do not share private machine, network, customer, certificate-subject, log, or account data.

## Suggested reviewer questions

These questions are offered to improve factual coverage, not to steer a positive answer:

1. What buyer problem does the reviewed workflow address, and for which type of venue?
2. What was actually installed and tested?
3. Which steps worked, failed, or required assistance?
4. What hardware and network conditions were required in this one environment?
5. Where did the workflow stop before the venue's fiscal process?
6. Which result is directly observed, and which statement came from POSPal?
7. What should another operator verify in their own 30-day trial?

## Publication-quality evidence checklist

An external source counts as a candidate for `FAC-IND-001` only when all applicable items are recorded:

| Check | Required record |
| --- | --- |
| Source control | Publisher controls the wording, conclusion, URL or retained report, and correction process |
| Attribution | Author/publisher and publication date are visible or privately verifiable |
| Disclosure | Supplied access, payment, discount, sample, or relationship is disclosed |
| Product identity | `POSPal.gr` and the tested version are unambiguous |
| Method | Environment and material steps are described sufficiently to interpret the result |
| Evidence | Direct observation is distinguishable from screenshots or facts supplied by POSPal |
| Scope | Limitations and untested areas prevent broad compatibility or outcome inference |
| Durability | Stable URL, archive, issue/date, or retained customer-approved record exists |
| Permission/privacy | People, venue, imagery, logs, and operational data have the needed permissions and redactions |
| Correction route | A factual correction can be requested without controlling the source's opinion |
