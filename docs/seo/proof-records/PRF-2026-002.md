## Customer selection gate

A candidate is suitable only when all conditions are true:

- it is a real hospitality business using or actively testing POSPal;
- its venue type matches an existing search/use-case page or supplies a distinct evidence-backed page job;
- it will permit publication of the business name;
- it will permit the exact environment, workflow, limitations, and selected evidence to be described;
- it understands that POSPal is not a fiscal POS or cash register;
- it can approve or reject every proposed quotation, figure, image, and attribution;
- it does not require fabricated anonymity, exaggerated results, or removal of material limitations;
- it may publish or confirm the setup in a channel it controls, if willing, without being given a required conclusion.

## Customer evidence request

Request only evidence required for the agreed claims:

1. approved public business identity and venue context;
2. exact Windows computer and POSPal version;
3. exact staff device, operating system, and browser/app;
4. router/access point and relevant local-network setup;
5. exact printer make, model, interface, driver, paper width, and firmware if printing is part of the record;
6. one installation or configured-start record;
7. one traceable test or operational order without guest personal data;
8. authentic kitchen or printer output when claimed;
9. failures, workarounds, untested conditions, and support intervention;
10. baseline and measurement method for any proposed numerical result;
11. the customer's own wording about the problem and experience;
12. independent customer-controlled URL, if the customer chooses to publish.

Do not request guest data, private employee data, account credentials, fiscal records, or unrelated commercial information.

## Public proof outline

The eventual Greek asset should follow this order:

1. answer the venue/use-case question immediately;
2. identify the named business and relevant operating context;
3. state the initial problem in approved factual language;
4. show the exact tested setup;
5. trace one real order through the relevant workflow;
6. state the observed result and method;
7. show limitations, workarounds, and what was not tested;
8. repeat the fiscal-POS boundary where relevant;
9. link to any customer-controlled or independent source;
10. offer `Κατέβασε για Windows` as the primary CTA and the exact setup guide as secondary.

## Page-creation gate

Do not create a new case-study URL automatically. First determine whether:

- the venue/use-case intent is real and distinct;
- an existing venue page can own the proof without confusing query ownership;
- the evidence is complete enough to exceed the current page quality bar;
- the customer permits a durable public record;
- the page can ship in a small measurable batch.

If any condition fails, retain the record internally or place an approved concise proof module on the appropriate existing unlocked page.

## Independent corroboration option

The customer may describe the setup on a website or social profile it controls. POSPal may supply factual material and reproduction steps, but must not require a positive conclusion, dictate a testimonial, or describe the source as independent when POSPal authored the publication.
